# Low-Rank Enemies

What bites in the dark of the village, the swamp, and the forest. Threats for early scenes: a night, a deal gone wrong, a walk home.

### The Twice-Buried
A dead villager buried wrong, or buried twice. Doesn't know it's dead. Comes home, knocks politely, waits at the threshold because it can't cross without permission. Say "come in" and you've let it in, and the terms are now yours.
- **Bite:** patience. It will wait all night. It has nowhere else to be.

### The Soured Domovoi
A house spirit whose house stopped feeding it. No longer protects, now punishes. Throws things, puts out the fire, hides the salt. Won't leave until the house is a home again.
- **Bite:** you can't fight it in its own house. You have to fix the house.

### The Kikimora (swamp)
Petty, house-proud, dangerous if disrespected. Gives wrong directions on purpose. If you insult her bog, the path loops back to her, and she's laughing.
- **Bite:** misdirection. You don't lose a fight, you lose the way.

### The Offended Rusalka
A water spirit with a grudge. Calls your name from the water. Trades fairly until you cheat her, then drags. Not evil; owed.
- **Bite:** you can't cross the water until you settle it.

### The Leshy's Decoy
A patch of forest the leshy made to be wrong. The same tree twice. Your own footprints coming toward you. A clearing that's the one you left.
- **Bite:** it costs time and nerve, not blood.

### The Clerk
An Office clerk who's been at his post too long and has become the paperwork. Grey, polite, unstoppable. Asks for a document you don't have. Can't be reasoned with; can only be out-waited or out-filed.
- **Bite:** bureaucracy as a haunting. The queue is the monster.

### The Static
A leak-ghost. A thing that charges wrongly: drains devices, then people, then memories. Attracted to the hum. Looks like interference in the air, a shimmer that eats the edges of things.
- **Bite:** proximity. Stay near it and you forget what you were doing. Stay longer and you forget who.

### The Borrowed Name
A reputation that got loose in the Market and now walks. Wears a name it rented and never returned. People treat it as whoever it's wearing. It doesn't want to give the name back.
- **Bite:** social. It will be believed over you.
