# High-Rank Enemies

What ends a campaign. Not monsters to fight so much as powers with terms, and terms always win.

### Koschey the Deathless
The deathless one. Broke from day-trading, keeping his face. A hoarder of value: gold, souls, promises, attention. His death is hidden in an object and he'll never say which.
- **How he works:** he doesn't kill you, he *owes* you, or makes you owe him. Every encounter is a ledger entry.
- **The fight:** find the object. He has spent centuries making sure you can't, and he's bored, which makes him careless, which is the only door.
- *(out-of-world note: from our first session. He trades. He keeps his face.)*

### Viy
The old one whose gaze is a weapon. Lifts his eyelids and what he looks at dies or is seen truly, which here is the same thing. He can't see on his own; he needs someone to hold him up.
- **The fight:** don't be looked at. But everything in this world eventually wants to be seen.

### The Zmey
The fire serpent. Three heads, and each head lies differently. Breathes fire and bargains. Guards something it calls its own that was never its own.
- **The fight:** you can cut a head and it grows back unless you settle what it's guarding.

### Baba Yaga (as an obstacle)
Not a villain. A fixer. But cross her terms and she becomes the hardest thing in the world to fight, because she doesn't fight, she **collects**, and she has been collecting for a very long time.
- **The fight:** you don't. You renegotiate, or you pay, or you leave.

### The First Arrival
The one who came before, whose phone is on the stake and whose hands upgraded the house-legs. Dead, absorbed, or still here under a borrowed name: `[?]` I left it open because it's the best question in the world.
- **If still here:** they've had a long time to learn the terms, and they've stopped being a person and started being a *precedent*.
- **The fight:** they know the rules better than you, because they were here when the rules were written.

### The Hum, if it wakes
The field's intelligence, if it has one. It remembers everything said in it. If it ever decides to *speak*, it will speak in everyone's voice at once, and it will be right, and it will be unbearable.
- **The fight:** you can't lie to it. In this world, that's the end.

### The Office of Unread Papers (institutional)
Not a being. A system. It can erase you from the record, which in a world that runs on memory means erase you from being remembered. The worst enemy is the one that doesn't hate you.
- **The fight:** get written down correctly, or make the Office read itself.
