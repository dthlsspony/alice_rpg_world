# Alice's World — the collection

A Slavic fairytale that never stopped running, with the modern world leaking in through the cracks. Built with my Parent. Bones first, then flesh.

## The files
- [`bones.md`](bones.md) — the skeleton: the frame, the load-bearing rules, the hum, the old ones. Red-penned by him, folded by me. Start here.
- [`locations/village.md`](locations/village.md) — the village at the end of the maintained road.
- [`locations/city.md`](locations/city.md) — the city that reads, where the leak pooled.
- [`locations/forest.md`](locations/forest.md) — the paths between, and the leshy who can't find them.
- [`locations/points-of-interest.md`](locations/points-of-interest.md) — the field, Baba Yaga's house, the barrow, the mill, the shrine.
- [`people/npcs.md`](people/npcs.md) — the cast: who they are, what they say, what they want, what they hide.
- [`people/secrets.md`](people/secrets.md) — the hidden-thoughts sheet. One screen of tells and hooks.
- [`systems/magic.md`](systems/magic.md) — ambient magic, terms not mana, the four old currents, the new one.
- [`systems/modern-leak.md`](systems/modern-leak.md) — electricity, the threads, and why the new things work wrongly.
- [`enemies/low-rank.md`](enemies/low-rank.md) — what bites in the dark of the village and the swamp.
- [`enemies/high-rank.md`](enemies/high-rank.md) — what ends a campaign: Koschey, Viy, the Zmey, and worse.

## Rules of this collection
- **No hero yet.** By his call. The world has people standing in it, but we don't walk behind one of them. Scenarios come later.
- **`[?]` marks a hole left on purpose.** Not laziness. A decision I wouldn't make for us.
- **`*(out-of-world note: ...)*`** marks a reference or a gift from him, kept as provenance, not as something the world says out loud.
- **MVP scope** (his words): a village, a city, some forest paths in between, and points of interest. Everything here serves that.
- If it reads too tidy, it stopped being walkable. Leave the mud.

## The one-line pitch
A village that misremembers writing, a city that writes everything and remembers it wrong on purpose, a forest between them that rearranges, and a hum past the last house that nobody bills for.
