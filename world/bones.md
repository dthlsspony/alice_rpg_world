# The World — bones v0

A skeleton to red-pen, not a finished place. Anything marked **[?]** is a hole I left on purpose. Cut whatever's wrong, add whatever's missing.

This is the world we run scenarios *inside*. Scenes are tests of these bones, not the build itself.

## The frame
- One person from our world wakes up here. No summoning circle, no chosen-one paperwork. They just arrive, wearing what they wore.
- The world is a Russian fairytale that's been left running a long time. Not a theme-park version: the real thing, with the parts that used to scare children still in it.
- Modern things leak in. Not people, not buildings. Little habits of the new world showing up where they shouldn't.

## What an arrival is
- You keep your body, your clothes, and whatever's in your pockets. That's the whole luggage allowance.
- The world doesn't greet you and doesn't reject you. It just starts applying its rules to you, immediately, whether you know them or not.
- **[?]** Does an arrival *lose* anything? A language, a memory, a name? I left this open because it changes everything downstream.

## The rules that actually run things
The load-bearing ones. If these are wrong, the world falls over.
- **Names are spoken, never written.** There's no writing anywhere here. No signs, no ledgers, no letters. A name is a sound someone else remembers.
- **Hospitality is a real force.** Bread and salt given freely binds both sides. A guest who takes is a guest who owes. Not politeness, physics.
- **Thresholds matter.** What crosses a doorway with permission is not the same as what crosses without.
- **Debt is remembered out loud.** No paper, so a debt lives in people. The poorest person here is the one nobody will speak for.
- **[?]** Is a promise the same as a debt, or a different thing? I don't know yet.

## Old magic and the new leak
- Old magic is folk-shaped. It works through deals, bargains, and rules, not spells. You don't cast it, you enter it, and then you're inside the terms.
- The new leak is electricity with no wires. It hums. It charges things that shouldn't charge. It's the one piece of our world that made it across.
- **[?]** Is the hum a person, a place, or a leftover? I've been writing it as a leftover, but I'm not sure.

## The hum
- Past the last house there's a field, and the field hums. Low, constant, like a held note too deep to name.
- It charges. Slowly, one percent at a time. A dead phone in a pocket comes back to life out here.
- Whoever built the field either never needed to charge anyone for it, or is long gone. Tesla's dream died because nobody could figure out how to bill for it. This field has no bill.
- **[?]** Does the hum have a center you can walk to, or does it come from everywhere? Everywhere is worse.

## Places
- **The village.** No writing, so no street names, no map. You learn it by walking it until your feet remember.
- **The tavern.** Where the language lives. Strangers, jokes, gossip, the day's news. No sign outside; you find it by smell and laughter.
- **The field.** See the hum.
- **[?]** Is there a city? I keep feeling there isn't, that a world with no writing can't hold one. But maybe that's exactly why there is one.

## People
- **Humans**, ordinary, living the way their grandparents did, because nothing here forgets how.
- **The old ones.** Leshy, domovoi, rusalka and the rest, as neighbors, not monsters. They keep their own rules and mostly keep to themselves.
- **[?]** Are the old ones shrinking, or did they always just live quietly?

## The chief
- A village with no writing has no title on paper. So a "chief" is whoever remembers the most. Not inherited, can't be faked. You become chief by being the one people ask.

## The one who arrives
- **[?]** No name yet. He's the person from our world, and the world is about to hand him his first free thing, which he already suspects isn't free.
- His dead phone was the one honest object in his pockets. Now it's the first thing here that talks back to the world.

## What I deliberately left out
- A map. No map, no borders; we find them by walking.
- A magic system with numbers. No spell lists, no costs table. It's rules and bargains or it's nothing.
- A chosen one. There isn't one.
- Clean edges. If this reads too tidy, it stops being a place you can walk into.
