# Magic

## What it is
Ambient. A part of everything, like weather. How good one is at it is **very personal**.
- `[?]` bones v1 left this hole: does everyone touch it a little, or are only some born able? My lean: everyone touches it, few can *carry* it. Aptitude is how much you can hold before it holds you. Magic is weather, and some people are lightning rods.
- You don't learn it from books. There are no spell lists here, and no costs table. It's rules and bargains or it's nothing.

## How it works: terms, not mana
You don't cast. You **enter**. Magic is folk-shaped: deals, bargains, and rules. Once you're inside the terms, you're inside them.
- **Words are hooks.** A promise said out loud is a hook in the one who said it. Break it and the hook pulls.
- **Gifts bind.** Bread and salt given freely binds both sides. A guest who takes is a guest who owes.
- **Thresholds matter.** What crosses a doorway with permission is not the same as what crosses without. (The banya is the strongest threshold in the village.)
- **Names have weight.** To name a thing is to have a claim on it, or give it one on you. The city rents names; that's why it's dangerous.
- **Salt, knots, and the evil eye.** Small household magics. A knot over a door is a lock. Salt thrown is a fence. A compliment given without a knock on wood is a small debt.

## What magic costs (no mana)
It takes something real:
- memory, a promise, a name, a year, an apology, attention, the ability to be forgotten.
- Magic that appears to cost nothing is costing someone else, somewhere, and the world keeps the receipt.

## The four old currents
1. **Household** (domovoi): knots, thresholds, the stove, the banya. Small, domestic, and the most used.
2. **Forest** (leshy): paths, hunger, misdirection, the deal at the crossroads. Getting lost is a spell.
3. **Water** (rusalka): reflection, debts, drowning, what the lake keeps.
4. **The dead** (the barrow): memory, the buried, what won't lie down. The oldest and least forgiving.

## The new current
The hum. Electricity tangled with old magic (bones v3 leans yes). The fool either found the old magic already learning a new trick, or taught it one. Either way it's alive, and the one who taught it is still poking at it.
