# The Modern Leak

The modern world didn't arrive here. It **leaked**. A little at a time, through the cracks, into a place that never asked for it. Nobody imported anything. Things just showed up, working, wrongly.

## Electricity — the hum
- Wild, out past the village: the field. Slow, constant, charges what shouldn't charge.
- Tamed, in the city: the grid, metered and sold from the Tower that Hums.
- Embedded, everywhere else: old ones wired their lives to it. Baba Yaga's legs, the domovoi's stove, the leshy's GPS. The old ones didn't adopt technology. They absorbed it, the way they absorb everything, and now it's part of their bodies and their arguments.

## The threads — the internet
- Not global. Local, and it hums. Reachable through **glass plates**: Baba Yaga's slab, the rows in the Thread-House.
- Messages travel as static and arrive as feelings. People talk to the glass and someone far away listens.
- **The threads remember.** That's the danger. This is a world that survives by forgetting, and the new thing doesn't forget anything.
- The village has no threads. Whether that's isolation or protection is `[?]` a hole. My lean: protection, and the village doesn't know it's being protected.

## The objects
- **Dead phones** as relics. They charge near the field, one percent at a time. One is lashed to a stake at the shrine and sometimes rings.
- **The plate** (Baba Yaga's). Glass, warm, an audience inside it.
- **The GPS** (the leshy's). It moves him. It's wrong. He trusts it anyway.
- **Tank-track legs** (Baba Yaga's house). Upgraded by someone before us, with a transform mode. Evidence of an earlier arrival's hands.

## The rules of the leak
1. Modern things don't come with manuals. They come with **habits**, and the habits are wrong for this world.
2. They work, badly, and the badness is where the story lives.
3. The old ones embed them without understanding them, and the misunderstanding is permanent.
4. The leak is one-way. Nothing goes back.

## `[?]` Where did it start
Three candidates, and I left all three alive: the first arrival (the one whose phone is on the stake), the fool (who mixes the two by hand), or the barrow (something buried that never stopped running). A scenario can pick.
